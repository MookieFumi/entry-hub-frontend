# Landing page

Scope: the public page a visitor without a session sees at `/`. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: office and Facilities managers evaluating EntryHub for their rooms. Action: try the demo (sign-in); GitHub is secondary. Proof: the product's own components and real rule texts running on the page with demonstration data, labelled as such. Constraints: no invented customers, prices, figures or testimonials; no photography; Spanish and English.

Memorable moment: marking a free cell on the hero planner and seeing your own highlighter band appear.

Unresolved: none (see Owner decisions).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one large planner sheet; its content lives in the sheet's cells and rows. Refuses the split hero with a screenshot and the row of equal feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: The office wall planner, as in the app: a drenched marker-ink blue sheet ruled with lighter grid lines, white laminate rows, a fluorescent yellow closing row; condensed heavy grotesque lettering; sticker colours with jobs (green free, coral busy, yellow yours).

STORY: The visitor understands that people book in a minute and the office's rules are enforced by the system; believes it because the planner and the real rule messages work in front of them; opens the demo.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Blue sheet. Header row with wordmark, section links, language and the demo button. A weekday header band, then the headline in condensed bold up to 6rem across the grid, one paragraph, primary button "Probar la demo" and secondary GitHub link. Directly below, still on the blue sheet, a working planner board: three demo rooms by office hours.

FORM: Planner poster, dealt lead (index 6 of 7); seed:1d14d462008c2550 (degraded roll). Code-led: no image generation here. Signature interaction: click a free cell to book it in highlighter; a busy cell or a sixth booking answers with the product's real rule message. Motion grammar: marker bands draw in from the left once; 150 ms state changes.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Owner decisions

- The primary action is "Probar la demo"; the owner chose it knowing that no pricing or commercial contact exists. Pricing and contact are omitted from the page until the owner defines them; no placeholder is shown to visitors.
- The headline is lettered over the sheet's column rules: this is the composition the owner picked ("titular gigante rotulado sobre la cuadrícula").
- mookie studio placement, confirmed by the owner: in the app, "un producto de" plus the lockup at full rail width at the foot of the rail; on the landing, the lockup large in its own band above the footer line.
